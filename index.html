Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how this app uses them, the types we use, and how you can manage your choices. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. Scope of this policy

This Cookie Policy applies to this application and any pages, features and services that link to it. It explains how we use cookies and similar technologies, and should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you use the app. They let the app remember your actions and preferences (such as sign-in and settings) over time, so you don't have to re-enter them each visit.

3. Similar technologies we use

Alongside cookies we may use local storage, session storage, pixels, software development kits (SDKs) and device identifiers. Where this policy says "cookies", it also covers these similar technologies.

4. Why we use cookies

  • To keep you signed in and keep the app secure.
  • To remember your preferences, like theme and selected views.
  • To understand how the app is used, so we can improve it.
  • To measure and, where you consent, personalise marketing.

5. Legal basis and your consent

We rely on your consent for preference, analytics and marketing cookies, which you give through the Privacy Settings screen. Essential cookies are used on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working service. You can withdraw consent at any time.

7. Essential cookies

These are necessary for the app to load, authenticate you and protect your session. Because they are strictly necessary, they cannot be switched off and do not require consent.

8. Preference cookies

These remember choices you make — such as language, theme, time zone and layout — so the app behaves the way you expect on your next visit.

9. Analytics cookies

These help us understand which features are used and where problems occur, using aggregated, non-identifying data. They are only set if you allow analytics.

10. Marketing cookies

These measure the performance of campaigns and, where permitted, help show you relevant messages. They are off by default and only set if you allow marketing.

11. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by trusted service providers acting on our behalf (for example analytics, security and payment providers). They may process limited data such as a device identifier or IP address to deliver their service, and are bound by contract to use it only for that purpose.

12. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close the app. Persistent cookies remain for the duration shown in the table above, unless you clear them sooner. We review retention periods regularly to keep them no longer than necessary.

13. Managing and disabling cookies

You can change your choices at any time from the Privacy Settings screen. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser or device settings. Disabling essential cookies may stop parts of the app from working.

14. Do Not Track and browser signals

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. Where required by law, we treat a recognised opt-out signal as a request to disable non-essential cookies for that browser.

15. International data transfers

Some providers may process data in countries outside your own. Where that happens, we use appropriate safeguards — such as standard contractual clauses — to protect your data in line with applicable law.

16. Data security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect the information cookies collect, including encryption in transit and restricted access. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

17. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age required by local law, and we do not knowingly use cookies to collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us so we can remove it.

18. Rewards for sharing data (optional)

Sharing analytics and marketing data is entirely optional. If you choose to opt in, you can earn a small reward for taking part. For example, you earn $1,000 for each day you are active with data sharing turned on. Rewards are paid as in-app credit (or to your linked payment account) once your balance reaches $13,000, usually within the first week of the following month.

You can turn data sharing off at any time from the Privacy Settings screen. Rewards stop accruing when you do, and turning it off never affects your access to the app. Participation is voluntary, is not required to use the service, and we do not pay for essential cookies.

19. Your rights, changes and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology, law or how the app works; for material changes we will ask for your consent again through the Privacy Settings screen.

Questions, or want to exercise your rights? Reach us at horizononline.com.
